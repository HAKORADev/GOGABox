# AGREEMENT — for the people who play on GOGABox

This is the END-USERS agreement. It lives in the REPO (not inside the
app) on purpose: you can read it before you install, and nothing here
is a click-through theatre. It reflects what GOGABox actually is: the
box is a platform, the games come from open sources, and the platform
itself takes no cut, no side and no custody of you.

## The plain terms

1. **The box ships empty.** No game comes with the app. Every game
   arrives from a source (the official repo, a community repo, a
   hobbyist repo, or a local file you imported). The platform hosts the
   OFFICIAL packages; community and hobbyist packages come from their
   own repos — the box fetches them directly from github, we never
   proxy, re-host or review them.

2. **Games are not a must.** Do not like a game = be away from it. The
   platform runs no moderation, no ratings board, no takedown queue and
   no "this is wrong for my country/religion/family" desk. Reports
   exist for BROKEN things (REPORTING.md), not for taste.

3. **The age door, exactly.** Games carry an age tag (3..+21) and
   content tags (horror, gambling, politics, porn, psycho, gore,
   nudity, illegal trading — honest metadata). Your profile's age
   number gates exactly one thing: the PLAY button. An unset age opens
   games up to +12. Discovery, downloading, importing and owning work
   at any age. The enforcement of what a minor may access is the
   household's job — the box gives you the honest labels and the one
   door; it does not raise anyone's kids.

4. **The wallet is local.** GOGACoins live in your box's save file.
   There is no payment, no purchase, no refund desk — the currency has
   no cash value and the platform sells nothing (zero ads, MIT).

5. **The saves are yours.** They live in the GOGAs tree (public storage
   on Android, next to the exe on Windows) — carry them, back them up,
   delete them. The platform keeps no copy and runs no telemetry.

6. **The LAN is peer-to-peer.** Sessions run between devices on your
   network (or through your own port-forwarding). No matchmaking
   servers, no chat logs, no accounts.

7. **Mods happen. By design.** Any package's data is plain files; the
   community is invited to remix (developers/MODDING.md). A modded game
   is your local copy behaving the way you made it behave — the
   platform ships no anti-tamper because it ships no hostility.

8. **Updates are yours to accept.** The box checks the official source
   for its own updates on a schedule, stages the download, and applies
   it the platform-honest way (Android: the system package installer
   after you confirm; Windows: the file replaces after you close the
   box). Nothing installs itself silently.

9. **You follow your laws.** The platform is neutral tooling (MIT).
   What you download, run, mod and share is bound by the law where you
   live — the platform gives no legal cover and takes no liability for
   your use, and the developers' side carries the same burden in
   developers/ACKNOWLEDGMENT.md.

10. **The official tier is small on purpose.** Official = the packages
    in THIS repo (HAKORADev/GOGABox). Everything else is somebody
    else's repo wearing the community or hobbyist badge. The badge is a
    trust display, not a warranty — read developers/DISCOVER.md.
