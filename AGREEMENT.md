# AGREEMENT

GOGABox is free, open-source software (MIT). By using it you agree to
the plain terms below — no fine print, no accounts, no servers.

**The box ships without games.** The application is a launcher and a
runtime; games are folders you add yourself. What you do with the
folders you add is your business.

**Your saves are yours.** Progress, coins and saves live in the box's
data and in the games' own folders — copy them, back them up, delete
them; nothing phones home because there is no home to phone.

**The age door is honesty, not enforcement.** A game carrying an age tag
grays its play button for profiles younger than the tag. The box has no
way to verify anyone's age and does not try; parents own the profile.

**The LAN is yours.** Multiplayer sessions run on your local network
between devices you control. The box never opens your games or your
files to the internet.

**GOGACoins are play money.** They have no value outside the box, cannot
be bought with real money, and exist to make the arcade feel honest.

**Everything here is open by construction.** The games are folders you
can open, read and change; the box is source code you can build. What
you build and share with it is between you and the people you share it
with.
